# Changelog

All notable changes to this project will be documented in this file.

## 1.0.0 - 2026-11-17

First release of laravel-quebec-retention: destroy or anonymize each column of personal information once its retention period ends (Quebec Law 25, s. 23), with tombstones that re-apply it after a backup restore. Part of the Quebec Law 25 suite for Laravel.
